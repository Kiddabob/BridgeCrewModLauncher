Bridge Crew launcher service

This independent launcher service runs on Kiddabob’s existing hosting. It provides accounts, published mod packs, friends and launcher rooms. It does not receive Meta, Steam or Ubisoft passwords and does not connect to the game.

Stored information

The service stores your chosen username and display name, a password hash, hashed recovery codes and session tokens, friend requests, blocks, reports, uploaded packs and temporary room membership. Publisher names, pack descriptions and public rooms are visible to other players. Friends-only and invitation-only rooms have server-enforced access rules.

Sessions expire after seven days. Room presence expires after two minutes without renewal. Expired room records are cleaned up after a day as requests arrive. Rate limiting stores keyed hashes of request identities rather than raw IP addresses in the application database. The hosting provider may keep its normal web-server request logs.

Your password is not stored as plaintext. Save your recovery code privately; it can reset your account password. The Windows launcher encrypts its saved session for your Windows user.

Publishing

Publishing requires the service owner’s approval. Published versions are immutable, and the owner can hide releases or disable accounts. Removing a release from this service cannot remove copies already downloaded by players. Mod descriptions are supplied by publishers; the service does not certify game compatibility or mod behavior.

For project support or to arrange account/data management with the operator, visit the project repository. Do not post passwords, session tokens or recovery codes in public issues.
